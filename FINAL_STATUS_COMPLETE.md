#### You Can:

✅ Mark posts as "Featured"

✅ Edit/delete posts anytime

✅ No coding needed!

#### How It Works:
1. Open Sanity Studio: http://localhost:3333
2. Click "Blog Post" → "Create new"
3. Write your post
4. Upload images
5. Toggle **"Featured Post"** to **ON**
6. Set **"Featured Priority"** to **100**
7. Click **"Publish"**
8. **Homepage automatically updates!**
